## Large Language Models for Emergency Triage and Clinical Decision Support
---
1. Project Overview
The objective of this project is to design and develop an Artificial Intelligence (AI)-powered Clinical 
Decision Support System (CDSS) using Large Language Models (LLMs) to assist healthcare professionals in 
emergency departments with patient triage, risk assessment, diagnosis support, and treatment 
recommendations. The system aims to improve the efficiency, accuracy, and consistency of emergency 
care by analyzing patient symptoms, medical history, laboratory reports, vital signs, and clinical guidelines 
to provide evidence-based recommendations in real time.
Emergency departments often face challenges such as overcrowding, limited medical staff, delayed 
decision-making, and high patient volumes. An LLM-based clinical assistant can support physicians and 
nurses by rapidly processing structured and unstructured clinical information, prioritizing patients based 
on the severity of their conditions, recommending diagnostic tests, suggesting treatment options, and 
generating clinical documentation. The system is intended to assist healthcare professionals rather than 
replace clinical judgment.
The project will integrate Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Electronic 
Health Records (EHRs), clinical guidelines, medical knowledge bases, and explainable AI to provide reliable 
and transparent clinical decision support.
